The narrator begins by describing the hole in the ground beneath a hill, in which a particular hobbit lives. 
The hole is highly comfortable: it has bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, and dining rooms for entertaining the hobbit’s many visitors. 
The best rooms in the house have a view of the garden outside the hobbit’s home.
